1. Final detector settings
Based on the eight experiments, my proposed settings are:
Parameter	Current	Proposed
Inference confidence	0.10	0.05
Publishing confidence	0.30	0.30
NMS IoU	0.45	0.50
Image size	640	640
Class-agnostic NMS	false	false

The 0.05 threshold retains low-confidence detections for ByteTrack's second association stage. The 0.30 publishing threshold remains separate, so low-confidence detections need not automatically become published vehicle observations.
